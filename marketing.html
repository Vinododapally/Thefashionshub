Questions

Everything you’re wondering.

Does the money go to my account?

Yes. Each shop connects its own Razorpay account, so customer payments are settled directly to that business’s bank account. Razorpay completes the business verification (KYC) for each account.

Can every customer use their own domain and logo?

Yes. A shop can run on its own domain (for example shop.theirbrand.com), and its admin can change the name, upload a logo and pick brand colours at any time.

Can I change a customer’s features later?

Anytime. Flip a feature on or off, or move a shop to a different plan, and it takes effect immediately. Switched-off features are hidden in the shop and blocked on the server.

Which products and business types work?

Fashion, electronics, home & kitchen, beauty, grocery, books, toys, sports, jewellery, pets, automotive, gifts, handicrafts and tools — and you can add your own categories. Regulated goods such as prescription medicines or alcohol need their own licences and checks, so talk to us first.

Is there a mobile app?

The storefront is mobile-first and works beautifully in any phone browser, with a bottom navigation bar like an app. A native iOS/Android app isn’t included today.

How is it hosted and delivered?

It is a standard web application (React and Python FastAPI) that ships with Docker files. You can host it on your own server or cloud account, so your data stays under your control.

Can customers pay with UPI apps like Google Pay and PhonePe?

Yes — UPI, cards, net banking and wallets are offered through Razorpay once the shop’s Razorpay account has UPI enabled. Cash on delivery is available as a separate option.
